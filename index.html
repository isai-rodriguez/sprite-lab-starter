Sprite Lab

A window, a film strip, and a robot named Byte.

Lab 1: The Film Strip

One picture holds every frame. A window shows one frame at a time.

Lab 2: Byte Walks

Hover a window (or Tab to it) to make Byte walk. Hold the mouse down to run.

Lab 3: The Stage

Byte walks across the stage forever. Hover the stage to pause.
